## `> whoami`

```text
🎓  MS in Data Science — Indiana University, Bloomington
💼  3+ Years in Data Analytics & Risk Modeling
🔬  Currently: Building AI Agents & Enterprise RAG Systems
🎯  Goal: Engineer intelligent systems that automate decision-making
⚡  Fun fact: I believe data cleaning is the most critical step in AI success
```

My journey started with a simple belief: **data without action is just noise.**

With over 3 years of hands-on experience in data analytics and risk modeling at **Evontex IT Solutions** and **Indiana University**, I started where every great data story begins — **asking the right questions**. I learned to clean messy datasets, build KPI dashboards, write complex SQL queries, uncover trends through EDA, and translate numbers into business strategy that stakeholders actually act on.

But dashboards and reports were never the finish line for me — I wanted systems that *think*.

That analytical foundation became the launchpad. Understanding *how* data behaves gave me the intuition to model it. I moved from descriptive analytics into predictive modeling and statistical inference, and then into **AI Engineering** — where I now build intelligent agents, advanced RAG architectures, and LLM-powered applications that don't just analyze data — they act on it.

> **The analyst in me asks "what does the data say?"**
> **The engineer in me builds systems that answer autonomously.**

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 What drives me</b></summary>
<br>

- **Data-Driven Decision Making** — Translating complex data into clear, actionable business insights
- **Analytical Rigor** — EDA, statistical testing, KPI tracking, and storytelling with data
- **Agentic AI** — Multi-agent systems that reason and execute autonomously
- **Advanced RAG** — Retrieval architectures with re-rankers for precision at scale
- **LLM Integration** — Production-grade AI applications with real-world APIs
- **Financial AI** — Investment agents using live market data
- **Data Sanitization** — Specialized techniques for hyperspectral & multispectral imagery
