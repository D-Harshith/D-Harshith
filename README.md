## `> whoami`

```text
🎓  MS in Data Science — Indiana University, Bloomington
🤖  AI Engineer — fine-tuning LLMs, building agents & production RAG systems
📊  3+ years in Data Analytics, BI & risk modeling (the foundation)
🔬  Currently: QLoRA fine-tuning, RLVR reasoning eval, LLM inference optimization
🎯  Goal: engineer intelligent systems that automate real decisions
⚡  Fun fact: I still believe data cleaning is the most critical step in AI success
```

I build **AI systems that act, not just analyze.** Today that means fine-tuning open LLMs, designing agentic workflows, shipping retrieval-augmented generation into production, and squeezing real latency and throughput out of model serving.

But I didn't start in AI Engineering — I started by **asking the right questions of data.** Years of cleaning messy datasets, writing complex SQL, building KPI dashboards, and running statistical analysis taught me *how data actually behaves*. That intuition is exactly what makes the AI work reliable: good models live or die on good data.

> **The analyst in me asks "what does the data say?"**
> **The engineer in me builds systems that answer autonomously.**

- **LLM Fine-Tuning** — QLoRA / PEFT on domain datasets, measured against held-out evals
- **Agentic AI** — multi-agent systems (LangGraph, CrewAI, MCP) that reason and execute
- **Advanced RAG** — retrieval architectures with re-rankers and vector search for precision at scale
- **LLM Reliability** — RLVR reasoning evaluation, inference optimization, hallucination control
- **Production Deployment** — FastAPI, Docker, AWS, Terraform infrastructure-as-code
- **Data-Driven Decisions** — EDA, statistical testing, KPI dashboards, storytelling with data
